        Which waters have the fish comes from Ontario's fish survey records and stocking records. Launches and shore spots come from Ontario's fishing access points (Open Government Licence – Ontario) and OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). A fish on record means surveys found it there, not that it's biting today. Access data can be out of date, and Ontario's was last checked in 2015. To search, the app sends a rough area (rounded to about 1 km) to these free services. Your catches show as orange dots and are never sent anywhere. Always check that the shore is public and read the regulations before you fish.
